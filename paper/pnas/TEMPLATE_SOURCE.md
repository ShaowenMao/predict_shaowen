# PNAS template provenance

The manuscript follows the official **Template for preparing your research report submission to PNAS using Overleaf (2025)**:

<https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/template-for-preparing-your-research-report-submission-to-pnas-using-overleaf-2025/whbdryzwztnd>

The Supporting Information follows the official **PNAS Template for Supplementary Information**:

<https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/pnas-template-for-supplementary-information/wqfsfqwyjtsd>

The PNAS class, bibliography, journal-abbreviation, and research-article style files were copied from release `v1.1.0` of `christopherkenny/pnas` at commit `7f635aa152b17d7c5a4592365d8cfdc61e949c27`. That repository republishes and adapts the LPPL-licensed files from the official Overleaf PNAS research-article template.

The Supporting Information style and logo were copied from `geoHeil/PNAS-Template-for-Supplementary-Information` at commit `78d92e12501104f5573b2ad28885ce6917051048`, a mirror of the LPPL-licensed official PNAS SI template.

`pnas-2025-compat.sty` is a project-local compatibility layer. It supplies
the `\articletype` and `\dataavail` commands used by the official 2025
example when those commands are absent from the vendored class release.

Template requirements and source structure were checked against the official Overleaf pages on 7 August 2026. Before submission, compare the vendored files and manuscript requirements with the current PNAS Author Center and refresh them if PNAS has issued a newer template.
